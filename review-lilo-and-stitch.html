My Movie Review of Live Action LILO and Stitch (June 2025)

It was a very cute, funny and action packed movie

It’s all about a blue alien creature named Stitch who befriends a young girl on Earth named LILO

They are both outcasts and they found each other

It’s a very sweet friendship story

Quite a bit of Peril so not for very young children below 5 years old 

Suitable for anybody over 5 years old 

LILO’s mom and dad are gone

She lives with her older sister Nani . Who’s her parental guardian 

I give it 10 out of 10 stars ⭐️ ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️

If you like sweet friendship stories with lots of actions , cuteness and laughs 

You will love ❤️ this movie 

I highly recommend for you to go and see it with people you love
